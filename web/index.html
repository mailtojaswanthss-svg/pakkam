THE STORY BEHIND PAKKAM

It started with a conversation.

A conversation with a grandfather about the everyday difficulty of reaching public services.

01 / A family conversation

I love my grandparents very much. One day, my grandfather told me how hard it was to access loans, read government messages and find his way through complicated portals on a mobile phone. That conversation stayed with me.

02 / A question we kept asking

Why should essential information be so difficult to reach?

My friend and I began imagining an assistant that could speak in a person's language, read documents aloud and make complicated information easier to understand.

03 / A name with a meaning

Pakkam means a page or side in Tamil.

It reflects our original idea: helping people read and understand important documents, one page at a time. Information should not be out of reach because reading or navigating a digital system is difficult.

OUR VISION

Make essential public information understandable and accessible to everyone.

Technology should make public services feel closer, clearer and more human.
