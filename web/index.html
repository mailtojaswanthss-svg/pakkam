01 · The conversation

We often think of technology as something that makes life easier. Faster payments, instant information, services at our fingertips.

But one day, I realised that not everyone experiences technology that way.

I love my grandparents. During one of our conversations, my grandfather spoke about something that had been troubling him. Accessing government services wasn’t easy. Understanding government messages was a struggle, and navigating mobile applications and government portals felt like an entirely different world.

The information existed. The services existed. But accessing them was another challenge altogether.

And that conversation stayed with me.

02 · The question

What if technology could speak their language?

A friend and I talked it through, and it came down to a simple question:

Why should someone struggle to understand a government document when technology could simply explain it to them?

What if an elderly person could ask a question in Tamil and receive an answer in Tamil, with the page of the official document shown right there?

What if accessing information didn’t require knowing how to navigate complicated websites or understand official language?

03 · How Pakkam was born

We didn’t want to build another portal that people had to learn how to use.

We wanted to build something people could simply talk to.

That’s how Pakkam was born.

I began building an assistant that reads official scheme documents and answers questions in people’s own languages: Tamil, Hindi and English, with Tamil-English mixed typing too.

An assistant that doesn’t just give an answer, but shows where that answer came from.

And most importantly, one that knows when the documents don’t contain an answer, and says so rather than pretending to know.

We started with the schemes we could check against official documents. There is more to cover, and we say so openly.

04 · The name

Why the name Pakkam?

பக்கம் — Pakkam. A page in Tamil.

The name comes from the simplest part of our original idea.

A government document is made of pages. Each page contains information that could affect someone’s life, but understanding it shouldn’t require specialist knowledge.

We wanted to make every page easier to understand.

05 · What it is for

More than an AI assistant.

Pakkam is about reducing the distance between people and the information they need: between a government scheme and the person who could benefit from it.

Because technology should adapt to people, not the other way around.

WHAT IT DOES

What Pakkam can do

Listens in your language

Tamil, Tanglish, Hindi or English, by voice or text, and answers out loud.

Shows the proof

Every answer opens the page of the official document, with the line highlighted.

Says "I don't know"

If the documents do not say it, Pakkam tells you instead of guessing.

Checks WhatsApp forwards

Finds false claims and scam signs like fake links and OTP requests.

Helps you prepare

Documents to carry, and a draft appeal note if an application was rejected.

Connects you to people

Scheme helplines, and a way to send your question to an officer.
