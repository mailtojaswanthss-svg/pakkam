THE STORY BEHIND PAKKAM

Pakkam means page.

A voice assistant that answers questions about welfare schemes from the official government documents, and shows you the exact page.

Why we built it

I love my grandparents very much. One day, my grandfather told me how hard it was to access loans, read government messages and find his way through complicated portals on a mobile phone. That conversation stayed with me.

The real rules for schemes like Magalir Urimai Thogai or PM-KISAN are written in long Government Orders that few people read. So families rely on WhatsApp forwards, middlemen or chatbots that sound sure but can be wrong. For someone's ₹1,000 a month, a confident wrong answer is worse than no answer.

What Pakkam can do

🎙️

Listens in your language

Tamil, Tanglish, Hindi or English, by voice or text, and answers out loud.

📄

Shows the proof

Every answer opens the page of the official document, with the line highlighted.

🙅

Says "I don't know"

If the documents do not say it, Pakkam tells you instead of guessing.

🛡️

Checks WhatsApp forwards

Finds false claims and scam signs like fake links and OTP requests.

📋

Helps you prepare

Documents to carry, and a draft appeal note if an application was rejected.

👤

Connects you to people

Scheme helplines, and a way to send your question to an officer.

How it answers

  1. 1

    You ask

    Gnani Prisma turns your voice into text, even mixed Tamil and English.

  2. 2

    Pakkam finds the page

    It searches 18 official government documents, never the open internet.

  3. 3

    It checks before it speaks

    Every sentence must point to a page and every number must be on it. Then Gnani Timbre reads the answer aloud.
