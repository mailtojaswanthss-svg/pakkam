# Barriers Welfare Beneficiaries Face in India / Tamil Nadu (learning about, applying for, receiving benefits)

Research date: 2 Nov 2026. Figures carry the date of the underlying data. Where a number is a political claim or from a secondary or aggregator source, that is flagged.

## 1. Digital access: phones, smartphones, internet, digital literacy, gender gap

### Takeaway
Most target users have *a* phone, but a large minority, skewed toward rural women, cannot use the internet or do anything beyond calls. Around 4 in 10 adults (and about half of rural adults) could not use the internet in 2022-23, and fewer than half could send an email. In Tamil Nadu, women's phone ownership runs ahead of their internet use. A voice-first channel that works over basic calls and in Tamil fits this gap.

### Cited Findings
- **NFHS-5 (2019-21), Tamil Nadu:** 74.6% of women aged 15-49 have a mobile phone they themselves use (Puducherry 82.8%). The national figure in the same reply is 53.9% (about 54%). — [Rajya Sabha PQ 259, 3 Feb 2023](https://rsdebate.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/740019/1/PQ_259_03022023_S22_p18_p20.pdf); [data.gov.in state-wise table](https://data.gov.in/resource/stateut-wise-details-percentages-women-between-age-15-49-years-who-have-mobile-phone-fifth)
- **NFHS-6 (published 2026), India, women 15-49:** mobile ownership rose from 54% (NFHS-5) to 63.6%, and "ever used internet" rose from 33.3% to 64.3%. Rural women's internet use went from 24.6% to 58.6%, against urban 51.8% to 77.3%. Rural women's mobile ownership is 57.4%, against 77.6% urban. Tamil Nadu is named as a state where women's mobile *ownership exceeds internet use* (no TN figures given). — [Factly/The News Minute, 3 Jul 2026](https://www.thenewsminute.com/news/womens-internet-use-nearly-doubles-but-access-gaps-remain). The source also gives 33% for the rural NFHS-5 figure in one place, so it is internally inconsistent.
- **Secondary summary for Tamil Nadu:** about 47% of TN women use the internet. About 69% of rural women own phones, but only 39% use the internet. It cites the Economic Survey 2025-26 and NFHS-5, but its provenance is unclear and it is not verified against the fact sheet. — [Usthadian summary](https://www.usthadian.com/womens-status-indicators-in-tamil-nadu)
- **NSSO CAMS 2022-23 (NSS 79th round, July 2022-June 2023, more than 3 lakh households, age 15+), India:**
  - 86.5% can use a mobile phone, but only 59.8% can use the internet (rural 53.6%, urban 74%).
  - 43.4% can send or receive email.
  - About 38% can do online banking (rural 30%).
  - Only 30% can search online, send email *and* bank online.
  - 94.6% have a bank account.
  - [ThePrint, 11 Oct 2024](https://theprint.in/india/50-of-indias-internet-users-dont-know-how-to-send-email-a-third-know-online-banking-govt-survey/2308129)
- **Tamil Nadu youth:** about 55% of youth in Tamil Nadu knew how to send an email with an attachment. This is a youth figure, not a rural-women figure. — [search snippet via ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/india-has-1-trillion-digital-dream-but-73-youth-lack-basic-email-skills-shows-nsso-survey/1467605)
- **GSMA Mobile Gender Gap Report 2025:** about 39% of women in India use mobile internet (Pakistan 45%), and 71% of Indian men own a mobile phone. The South Asia mobile internet gender gap is 32%. Globally, about 885 million women in low- and middle-income countries are not on mobile internet. — [Dawn, citing GSMA 2025](https://www.dawn.com/news/amp/1911146); [GSMA report](https://www.gsma.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/The-Mobile-Gender-Gap-Report-2025.pdf)
- **How people use the internet (IAMAI-Kantar, 2023 users):** 86% use OTT streaming and 70% use social media, but only 45% use digital payments. Banking and payments take just 3-4% of phone time (BCG, Feb 2024). — [ThePrint, 11 Oct 2024](https://theprint.in/india/50-of-indias-internet-users-dont-know-how-to-send-email-a-third-know-online-banking-govt-survey/2308129)

### Inferences
- "Has a phone" is not the same as "can use a portal." The 27-point gap between phone use (86.5%) and internet use (59.8%) in CAMS, and the 70% of adults who cannot complete basic online tasks, describe users who can make and answer calls and use WhatsApp voice notes but cannot fill a web form. A Tamil voice interface, including over IVR or phone calls, reaches them; a text app or portal does not.
- Phone sharing within households (Factly notes some women go online through shared devices) means the woman applicant may not control the phone that receives the KMUT SMS or OTP.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve TN-specific NFHS-5 or NFHS-6 "women ever used internet" values; the fact sheet PDF link returned a 404. The IIPS TN fact sheet should be checked.
- No reliable TN-specific feature-phone vs smartphone split for rural women, the elderly or farmers. TRAI reports subscriptions, not device type. GSMA's India smartphone-by-gender figure was not in the sources I retrieved.
- CAMS gender-wise digital-skill tables for TN were not retrieved.

## 2. Awareness and misunderstanding of schemes (KMUT, PM-KISAN, Ujjwala)

### Takeaway
Large numbers of eligible or enrolled people fall out at the "understand rules / stay enrolled" stage rather than at first awareness:
- About 57 lakh KMUT applications were rejected (opposition figure), with no clear itemised reasons.
- Lakhs of PM-KISAN farmers lost instalments because of e-KYC and seeding rules they did not know about.
- Ujjwala refill drop-off is in the crores.

### Cited Findings
- **KMUT scale:** 1.63 crore applications were received in 2023. — [News Today, 22 Aug 2023](https://newstodaynet.com/2023/08/22/1-63-cr-applications-received-for-magalir-urimai-thogai-scheme/)
- **KMUT rejections:** PMK founder S. Ramadoss said only 1.06 crore were accepted and about 57 lakh were rejected "with no clarification from the government about the reason." This is a political claim, not an official tally. — [DT Next, Sep 2023](https://www.dtnext.in/amp/story/news/tamilnadu/provide-rs-1000-monthly-dole-to-all-women-in-tn-ramadoss-738117) (as summarised in search)
- **KMUT beneficiaries:** about 1,15,27,172 women by 31 Mar 2024. Rs 11,323 crore was disbursed to 1.16 crore women over 10 months. The FY 2024-25 sanction was Rs 13,722.47 crore. — [DT Next, 28 Jun 2024](https://www.dtnext.in/amp/story/news/tamilnadu/148l-appeals-accepted-under-kalaignar-mahalir-urimai-thittam-informs-udhayanidhi-792266)
- **KMUT confusion after launch (Vellore, Sep 2023):**
  - Help desks, banks and e-Sevai centres were flooded with complaints.
  - One eligible man's money had gone to a post-office account he said he did not have.
  - Rumours spread that banks would deduct from the Rs 1,000. Banks were in fact deducting charges because of how their systems were configured, until a minister warned them.
  - Banks were overcrowded after the SMS alerts.
  - [DT Next, 25 Sep 2023](https://www.dtnext.in/news/tamilnadu/deluged-with-complaints-on-kmut-scheme-claim-officials-738209)
- **KMUT still left out in 2025:** the state ran "Ungaludan Stalin" camps from 15 Jul 2025. About 10,000 camps were planned (3,768 urban, 6,232 rural), with decisions promised within 45 days. On day one in Chennai, 7,518 of 10,949 petitions were KMUT applications from women left out. — [The Week/PTI, 5 Jul 2025](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/07/05/mds17-tn-govt-camps.html); [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/amp/story/news/chennai/almost-11000-applications-submitted-at-ungaludan-stalin-camp-held-in-7-places-840232)
- **PM-KISAN exclusion by procedure:**
  - Aadhaar-based payment became mandatory from the 13th instalment, and e-KYC from the 15th (Apr-Jul 2023).
  - Punjab beneficiaries fell from 17.59 lakh (2021-22) to 7.97 lakh (2024-25), which the minister linked to land seeding, e-KYC and Aadhaar-bank linking.
  - [Rajya Sabha PQ 265, 26 Jul 2024](https://rsdebate.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/749525/1/PQ_265_26072024_S49_p40_p45.pdf)
  - In one state, 6.65 lakh farmers lost benefits for not updating e-KYC (2022). — [Deccan Chronicle, 6 Dec 2022](https://deccanchronicle.com/nation/current-affairs/061222/665-lakh-farmers-lose-pm-kisan-scheme-benefits.html)
  - About 70 lakh fewer farmers received the 21st instalment than the 20th after a verification drive. This comes from an aggregator and is not verified with a primary source. — [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/government-schemes/pm-kisan-update-govt-removes-70-lakh-farmers-from-21st-instalment-list-check-if-your-rs-2000-is-credited)
- **Ujjwala refill drop-off:**
  - In FY 2021-22, at least 90 lakh beneficiaries took no refill (RTI to oil companies); the government's own figure was 92 lakh. Over 1 crore refilled only once. — [Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/energy/ujjwala-over-9-million-beneficiaries-did-not-refill-cylinder-last-year-centre-admits-84130)
  - Lok Sabha 2024: of 10.33 crore beneficiaries, 1.54 crore took only one refill in FY 2022-23 and 1.66 crore in FY 2023-24, a rising share. — [The South First](https://thesouthfirst.com/news/rising-dropout-rate-in-pradhan-mantri-ujjwala-yojana-a-growing-concern/)
  - In FY 2022-23, about 12% of beneficiaries did not get a single refill. — [The Wire](https://m.thewire.in/article/government/in-fy23-lpg-refills-under-ujjwala-fell-12-of-beneficiaries-didnt-get-a-single-refill-govt/amp)
- **Information need (small sample):** a 2021 Haqdarshak survey of about 140 Uber drivers found 61% wanted more information on schemes they could apply for, and 45% or more had difficulty submitting forms. — [Haqdarshak via search summary](https://haqdarshak.com/?p=9509)

### Inferences
- The awareness problem is mostly about *procedural* awareness: e-KYC deadlines, seeding status, why an application was rejected, where the money went. Basic awareness that a scheme exists is less of an issue. A voice assistant that explains a person's status and the next step in Tamil addresses the Vellore-style confusion directly.
- Ujjwala drop-off is mainly about affordability, so information alone will not fix it. The voice-assistant angle is limited to subsidy and refill-entitlement awareness.

### Gaps
- No official itemised KMUT rejection count by reason was found.
- No TN-specific PM-KISAN exclusion numbers were found.
- No survey measuring the share of *eligible* TN women unaware of KMUT was found.

## 3. Rejection reasons, documentation problems and the KMUT appeal process

### Takeaway
Rejections cluster around documentation and data-matching:
- Aadhaar-bank and NPCI seeding
- Land records for PM-KISAN
- Income and eligibility cross-checks against government databases for KMUT
- Form errors

KMUT's appeal path relied on an SMS reason, a 30-day window and an e-Sevai or portal filing. 9.24 lakh appeals were filed and 1.48 lakh accepted.

### Cited Findings
- **KMUT appeal mechanics:** rejection SMSes with reasons went out on 18 Sep 2023, and appeals were allowed within 30 days of the SMS. The new portal kmut.tn.gov.in crashed on the first morning of appeals (19 Sep 2023) "owing to the enormity of traffic." — [DT Next, 19 Sep 2023](https://www.dtnext.in/news/tamilnadu/kmut-website-reportedly-crashes-on-day-one-of-appeal-737308)
- **Where to appeal:** sources differ. Some say the office of the Revenue Divisional Officer (RDO); others say the nearest e-Sevai centre. — [Zee News Tamil](https://zeenews.india.com/tamil/photo-gallery/kalaingar-magalir-urimai-thogai-how-to-appeal-if-your-application-is-rejected-586779); [govtschemes.in](https://www.govtschemes.in/comment/63165)
- **Appeal volumes:**
  - Over 9.24 lakh women filed appeals. — [inkl / The Hindu syndication](https://inkl.com/news/9-24-lakh-women-file-appeals-against-their-rejection-from-kalaignar-magalir-urimai-thittam)
  - 11.85 lakh women resubmitted applications through e-Sevai, with a deadline of 24 Oct 2023. — [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/news/tamilnadu/1185-lakh-women-resubmit-applications-to-avail-urimai-thogai-says-udhayanidhi-744415)
  - 1.48 lakh appeals were accepted, as reported in the June 2024 policy note. — [DT Next, 28 Jun 2024](https://www.dtnext.in/amp/story/news/tamilnadu/148l-appeals-accepted-under-kalaignar-mahalir-urimai-thittam-informs-udhayanidhi-792266)
  - My calculation: about 16% of those appeals were accepted, comparing these two figures across sources.
- **KMUT backlog:** at one point 1,07,910 applications were under scrutiny. Of these, 55,160 were pending with the Tamil Nadu e-Governance Agency (TNeGA) and 52,750 were in field verification. Special camps were held at e-Sevai centres for women who missed payments. — [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/amp/story/news/tamilnadu/special-camps-on-at-e-sevai-centres-says-min-738742)
- **KMUT rejection reasons:** the government cited "deficiencies in the application form." An unofficial 2026 guide lists Aadhaar not linked to the bank account as common. — [search summary, govtschemes / egovtschemes](https://www.egovtschemes.com/tamil-nadu-magalir-urimai-thogai/). These sources are weak.
- **Dvara Research, "Delivery of G2C Cash Transfers in India" (released June 2022), all-India including TN:** grievance analysis of 1,017 IVR complaints (Gram Vaani: UP, MP, Bihar, Jharkhand, TN) covering MGNREGA, PDS and EPF. — [ThePrint, 4 Jun 2022](https://theprint.in/india/despite-direct-benefit-transfers-welfare-schemes-beneficiaries-still-floundering-claims-study/983113)
  - Application and document issues made up about 72% of grievances.
  - 32%: enrolment centre too far or official unavailable.
  - 22%: application took too long to be considered or accepted.
  - 17%: difficulty getting documents.
  - 13%: repeat visits, with extra cost and lost time.
  - 9%: bank, Aadhaar or NPCI seeding problems.
  - 3%: form errors.
  - In the Dvara-Haqdarshak sample of 1,500 people with DBT problems, about 51% had payment disruptions (stopped without notice, missed instalments). The report notes a lack of clear communication to beneficiaries.
- **Haqdarshak, Annual Impact Report 2023:** barriers to identity documents include illiteracy, the digital divide, fees, lack of a fixed address, complex paperwork and unclear eligibility rules. Older women have lower documentation rates. — [Haqdarshak](https://haqdarshak.com/?p=9509)
- **Delhi widow pension RCT:** help with the application itself raised uptake more than information alone. Paperwork and procedural complexity were the main deterrents. — [summary via search; NextBillion / BasisPoint](https://basispointinsight.com/Story/simpler-applications-can-lift-welfare-benefit-uptake_b7a5fcc8de02.html). Secondary summary; check the original paper.

### Inferences
- The highest-value assistant features are:
  - Decoding the rejection SMS: what the reason means and which document to fix.
  - A document checklist before going to e-Sevai, which cuts the 13% repeat-visit problem.
  - Reminders of appeal deadlines (30 days) and e-KYC requirements.
  - Explaining "payment stopped" states.
- The Dvara finding that payment disruptions come "without clear communication" is a direct, evidence-backed gap for a status-explaining assistant.

### Gaps
- No ration-card-mismatch or income-certificate rejection statistics for TN were found.
- No official breakdown of KMUT appeal outcomes by reason was found.

## 4. Misinformation and fraud using scheme names

### Takeaway
Fake scheme messages are frequent and specific:
- Fake "amount hike" or bonus instalments
- Fake "scheme stopped" claims
- Recycled old application dates
- Fake registration links and APKs

In Tamil Nadu, Newschecker Tamil has debunked several KMUT-specific fakes. Official guidance is "only gov.in / nic.in, CSC or tehsil office."

### Cited Findings
- **KMUT fakes debunked by Newschecker Tamil:**
  - Old August 2023 news cards about 18-20 August application dates were recirculated in 2024 as current. Minister Geetha Jeevan clarified they were false.
  - An edited news card claimed a politician (C.V. Shanmugam) had sued to stop the scheme.
  - Claims that the Rs 1,000 was stopped for 80 lakh women, or that the scheme was cancelled.
  - A fake claim that a TVK case had been filed in the Supreme Court against early disbursement.
  - Sources: [Newschecker TA – magalir urimai thogai news](https://newschecker.in/ta/fact-check-ta/magalir-urimai-thogai-news); [stopped claim](https://newschecker.in/ta/fact-check-ta/magalir-urimai-thogai-stopped); [edited news card](https://newschecker.in/ta/fact-check-ta/cv-shanmugam-edited-news-card); [TVK case](https://newschecker.in/ta/fact-check-ta/kalaignar-urimaithogai-tvk-case)
  - The verdicts on the "80 lakh stopped" and "scheme cancelled" posts were not fully visible in the results.
- **PM-KISAN fakes:**
  - PIB Fact Check flagged as false a viral "extra ₹4,000 Raksha Bandhan instalment." — [CareerIndia](https://www.careerindia.com/features/pm-kisan-4000-raksha-bandhan-grant-fact-check-viral-ai-videos-fake-claims-011-64505.html)
  - PIB flagged fake "money deposited under yojana" SMSes, including ones about PM-KISAN instalments. — [Factly](https://factly.in/government-is-not-associated-with-this-cash-deposit-sms-its-fake)
  - A "PM-KISAN ₹10,000" claim was false; the benefit is still ₹6,000. — [indianpaycalculator](https://www.indianpaycalculator.in/govt-news/pm-kisan-10000-truth-still-6000-24th-installment-2026)
  - A fake "PM Kisan tractor scheme" was debunked by PIB. — [Krishi Jagran](https://krishijagran.com/news/fact-check-no-pradhan-mantri-kisan-tractor-scheme-pib-debunks-viral-message)
- **Ministry warning:** the Ministry of Agriculture warned of "false promises of additional benefits, fake registration links, or misleading updates." It said PM-KISAN registration happens only via pmkisan.gov.in, a CSC, or the tehsil agriculture office. — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/ministry-of-agriculture-warns-about-fraudulent-messages-under-the-guise-of-pradhan-mantri-kisan-samman-nidhi-scheme); [LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/india/news/pm-kisan-scheme-fraudsters-using-fake-links-messages-and-calls-to-dupe-farmers-check-list-of-official-websites-and-20th-installment-date-update-7018068.html/amp)
- **Malicious APKs:** Rajasthan Police warned of fake PM-KISAN APKs and links shared on social media that steal data or money. — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/rajasthan-police-warns-against-fake-pm-kisan-apps-links/2650568/). Dates may be uncertain.
- **Other schemes:** PIB declared PM-KUSUM registration websites fraudulent, and people received fake PM-KUSUM approval letters. — [LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/social-viral/fact-check/websites-inviting-registration-under-pm-kusum-scheme-are-fraudulent-clarifies-pib-fact-check-as-whatsapp-message-goes-viral-1880787.html); [Inshorts](https://inshorts.com/en/news/people-receiving-fake-approval-letters-under-pm-kusum-scheme-1732636917972)
- **Benefit-related rumours as a harm:** the Vellore rumour that banks would deduct KMUT money (2023). — [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/news/tamilnadu/deluged-with-complaints-on-kmut-scheme-claim-officials-738209)

### Inferences
- The common pattern is "₹X hike / new instalment / scheme stopped / apply by date." An assistant that answers "Is this message true?" against the official scheme data (current amount, dates, official portals) would address a recurring, documented harm.
- A built-in "never pay or share an OTP; official domains end in gov.in or nic.in" guardrail matches the official guidance.

### Gaps
- No quantified cyber-fraud loss figures tied to scheme names in TN.
- No Tamil Nadu Fact Check Unit-specific posts were found.
- No documented TN cases of middlemen charging KMUT applicants were found in this search.

## 5. How people seek help today: e-Sevai, officials, helplines, camps, and their costs

### Takeaway
Help-seeking runs through physical intermediaries:
- e-Sevai and CSC centres
- Village and taluk officials
- Collectorate help desks
- Mass government camps

These involve distance, repeat visits, crowds, and fees in private centres that can be about 10x the official rate. Helplines exist (e.g. an e-Sevai complaint toll-free number), but evidence of their reach is thin.

### Cited Findings
- **Overcharging at private e-Sevai centres:**
  - A minister warned private e-Seva centres that operators who charge extra will lose their user ID, and gave toll-free 18004252911 for complaints (article undated). — [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/news/tamilnadu/strict-actions-if-pvt-eseva-centres-charge-more-minister-udumalai-radhakrishnan)
  - In Chennai, a birth certificate costs Rs 20 at a public centre but can cost Rs 200 at a private one. One private operator charges a minimum of Rs 250 per certificate (anecdotal). — [Citizen Matters](https://citizenmatters.in/e-sevai-centres-chennai-data-security-certificates-governance/)
  - The official service charge for learner's licences at e-Sevai is Rs 60, set "to avoid paying a hefty sum to middlemen." — [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/news/tamilnadu/e-sevai-centres-to-issue-llr-from-today-773660)
- **Intermediaries as help desks:** after KMUT rejections, complainants were sent to e-Sevai centres, which then had to explain the system. Collectorate help desks were flooded. — [DT Next, 25 Sep 2023](https://www.dtnext.in/news/tamilnadu/deluged-with-complaints-on-kmut-scheme-claim-officials-738209)
- **Mass camps:** the "Ungaludan Stalin" campaign (Jul-Nov 2025) planned about 10,000 camps. A DT Next headline reports 30 lakh petitions in 30 days across services (not verified against a primary source). — [DT Next topic page](https://www.dtnext.in/topic/slug-430973); [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/07/05/mds17-tn-govt-camps.html)
- **Distance and repeat visits (Dvara 2022):**
  - 32% of grievances concern centres being too far or officials unavailable; 13% concern repeat visits.
  - At cash-out: 46.3% said the bank was too far, 49.2% the ATM, and 52.6% the business correspondent.
  - About 40% cited network failures.
  - [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/despite-direct-benefit-transfers-welfare-schemes-beneficiaries-still-floundering-claims-study/983113)
- **IVR as a grievance channel:** Dvara's grievance data itself came from Gram Vaani's interactive voice response platform, including in TN, which shows that rural users will use voice and phone channels to report problems. — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/despite-direct-benefit-transfers-welfare-schemes-beneficiaries-still-floundering-claims-study/983113)
- **e-Sevai service experience:** Indus Action published an e-Sevai case study in June 2026. Not reviewed in detail. — [Indus Action PDF](https://indusaction.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/e-Sevai-Case-Study_04.06.26.pdf)

### Inferences
- A voice assistant cannot replace e-Sevai for submission, but it can cut wasted trips (document checklist, status checks, rejection decoding) and flag fair fees (public centre rates), reducing the middleman premium.
- The Gram Vaani precedent supports an IVR or phone-call channel for users without smartphones.

### Gaps
- No data was found on usage or answer rates of the CM Helpline 1100 (Mudhalvarin Mugavari) or on waiting times at e-Sevai.
- No systematic data on how often people rely on relatives or intermediaries, beyond the Dvara and Haqdarshak samples.
- The Indus Action e-Sevai case study (2026) should be read for TN-specific wait-time and cost data.
- LibTech India and J-PAL South Asia TN-specific findings were not retrieved in this pass.
